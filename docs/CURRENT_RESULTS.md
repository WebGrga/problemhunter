## What improved and what was discarded

Visible replies are separately attributed and dated, bounded to eight selected
replies from at most 40 visible post elements. Quotes, repeated answers and
unattributed replies are excluded. Replies never become the original speaker's
complaint or additional independent complainants. Review distinguishes original
complainant resolution, reported remedies, rejected workarounds and continuing
failures. Confirmed original-author resolution removes the current lead; vendor
claims and other people's fixes require review. Partial-page coverage and skipped
replies remain explicit limits. Promotion can still be misread as a remedy report;
this label never verifies a present-day solution.

A cached replay of the earlier Shopify/Square samples verified context behavior.
The bounded general-v5 grouper combined two cross-channel overselling complaints
and kept the different manual-sync burden separate. Every pair in a group must
match the same specific failure/task; uncertain and untested pairs stay separate.
It considers at most 16 candidate pairs. This is one measured grouping success,
not demonstrated general grouping accuracy. No extra recurrence appeared in the
four fresh samples. Existing production/v4 grouping is unchanged.

Automatic workflow briefs were tried and removed. Literal fragments gave vague
queries, unrelated complaints were combined, and a button/feature name could be
classified as a platform. The resulting restaurant comparison was partial and
returns research drifted toward subscription cancellation. Keeping this path would
make an ordinary command look more complete without making its result useful.
General solution research therefore still needs a reviewed person/task/outcome/
constraint brief. Current unmet need and AI/native remedies remain unverified.
