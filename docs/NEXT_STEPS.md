# Next steps

Problem Hunter is not yet reliable enough to recommend what business to build.
The current bottleneck is complete, attributable, current complaint evidence,
followed by correctly separating people experiencing problems from solution makers
and commenters. See [CURRENT_RESULTS.md](CURRENT_RESULTS.md).

1. Improve permitted full-body acquisition on one narrow workflow. Measure complete
   attributed posts, correct actor/task matches and independently repeated unresolved
   problems. Do not bypass access restrictions or expand the crawl to mask poor yield.
2. Review selected complaints against original posts and present-day remedies.
   Mixed snippets and maker descriptions must not become apparently strong leads.
   A ranking score is not accuracy, demand or current unmet need.
3. Complete existing-remedy research only for a credible problem. Automatic
   source-backed workflow definition needs evaluation before it can replace the
   manual brief. Do not invent a constraint to force the process to continue.
4. Compare the next change on a separate fresh sample with the same bounded
   settings. Preserve the baseline and report useful outcomes, failures and actual
   estimated cost. Do not retune questions for each industry or call a replay a
   fresh validation.

No new dashboards, scoring dimensions, providers or large test/doc collections
until a change improves this measured outcome. Retain tests that protect real
behavior; remove them when their obsolete feature is removed.

The shared $1 budget has $0.628407472 remaining after October 11. Record incremental
cost and enforce the cumulative limit manually across runs. Do not spend further
merely to produce a more impressive-looking result.

The first collection repair is implemented: single-post extraction on recognized
forum layouts and original-date exclusion before evaluation. A live broader-search
trial still found no usable current full discussions, so that search change was
discarded. The next acquisition work needs a permitted source of current complete
posts, such as official API access or an authorized export, with attribution and
dates intact. Do not keep expanding web queries when access is the limiting factor.
