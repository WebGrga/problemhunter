# Existing-remedy research

General research requires a reviewed brief describing the affected person, task,
desired outcome and remaining constraint. Topic selection alone does not establish
that workflow. This manual requirement is an unresolved automation gap.

Standalone `research` saves the brief/search plan and compares named remedies
without reevaluating or regrouping the underlying complaints. Software, services,
physical products, process changes and community approaches are supported.
Remedies named in the source can be supplied as `known_remedies`.

The current comparison uses separate identity, subject-evidence and quote-assessment
calls. Function, pricing, setup, limitations, customer experience, adoption and task
fit retain literal excerpts and source provenance. Provider claims, third-party
accounts and review/download counts have different meanings. Partial task fit
does not establish that a compound remaining constraint is satisfied.

Results are `apparently_well_served`, `plausible_residual_gap` or
`insufficient_information`. They are research interpretations, not business
validation. Search is bounded and incomplete. Exact quotes establish literal
presence, not correct interpretation, current pricing or real-world performance.
Changing the brief or supporting problem set invalidates old comparison results.

The latest live client-feedback comparison found five named remedies and 25 claims,
but complete constraint fit remained unknown. No verified customer complaints or
adoption evidence established demand. See [CURRENT_RESULTS.md](CURRENT_RESULTS.md).
Older parser compatibility tests remain because they exercise provenance and
identity checks; passing them does not establish semantic accuracy.
